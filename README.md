# Jessica's English Learning Guide - To Students and Adults of All Ages 🎓

A modern, interactive, and pedagogically rich Streamlit web platform featuring **Teacher Jessica**, designed for English learners of all ages—from young learners and complete beginners to middle/high school and university students, adult professionals, and lifelong learners.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 👩‍🏫 Teacher Jessica Spotlight
- **Personalized Mentorship**: Includes Teacher Jessica's real portrait embedded across the platform, including the sidebar profile, the Welcome Spotlight, the Complete Learning Guide header, simulated dialogue roleplays, and writing diagnostics.
- **Interactive Audio Guidance**: Listen to Teacher Jessica's personal welcome message and lesson audio.

### 1. 🐣 Beginners & Young Learners (A1 - A2)
- **Phonics & Tricky Sounds**: Master short vs. long vowels, consonant digraphs (voiced vs. voiceless `TH`, `CH` vs. `SH`), and silent letters with IPA transcripts and audio pronunciation.
- **Visual & Everyday Vocabulary**: Categorized themes (greetings, family, food, emotions, directions) with clear audio.
- **Interactive Sentence Builder**: Visual hands-on constructor based on the Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) model with read-aloud functionality.

### 2. 📚 Students & Academic Grammar (B1 - B2)
- **The Complete 12 English Tenses Matrix**: Comprehensive breakdown of formulas, affirmative/negative/question structures, signal words, and Jessica's trap warnings.
- **Core Grammar Demystified**: The In/On/At preposition pyramid, Conditionals (Zero, 1st, 2nd, 3rd), Active vs. Passive voice, and Subject-Verb agreement.
- **Academic Essay Writing Lab**: Master the **PEEL** paragraph method (Point, Explanation, Evidence, Link) and high-scoring transition words.
- **Confusing Words & Homophones**: Solve pairs like *affect vs. effect*, *their/there/they're*, *advice vs. advise*, *loose vs. lose*, and *principal vs. principle* with mnemonic tricks.

### 3. 💼 Adults & Career English (B2 - C1)
- **Business Email Builder**: Customizable, copy-ready email templates for follow-ups, deadline extensions, polite declines, and professional apologies.
- **Casual vs. Formal Language Transformer**: Direct comparisons teaching learners how to phrase everyday thoughts with executive polish.
- **Executive Meeting Phrases**: Tactful disagreement, interrupting courteously, asking for clarification, and summarizing action points.
- **Job Interview Masterclass**: Answering tough behavioral questions using the **STAR Method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

### 4. 💡 Idioms, Slang & Cultural Expressions
- Curated collection of classic and modern English idioms with literal vs. figurative meanings, example sentences, and historical origins.

### 5. 🎯 Interactive Quiz & Assessment Lab
- Multi-tier questions spanning beginner foundations, grammar tenses, confusing words, and workplace English.
- Instant scoring, live progress indicators, and detailed pedagogical explanations for every option.

### 6. 🎙️ Speaking & Pronunciation Studio
- Interactive Text-to-Speech playground: Type any custom sentence, select accent (American, British, Australian), and adjust speed (0.7x to 1.2x) for shadowing practice.
- Diction & Tongue Twister challenges.

### 7. ✍️ Smart Writing & Grammar Polisher
- Learner draft diagnostic: Identifies tense inconsistencies, redundant prepositions, and subject-verb disagreements, providing a natural, polished alternative and teacher tips.

### 8. 💬 Real-World Conversation Roleplay
- Interactive scenarios (Coffee shop, Airport check-in, Job interview, Doctor's clinic) with simulated teacher replies and coaching feedback.

### 9. 📑 Personalized Study Planner & Downloadable Reference
- 30-day milestone roadmap generator customized by daily commitment time and focus area.
- One-click exportable Markdown reference sheet.

## 🚀 How to Run the Website

### Step 1: Navigate to the Project Directory
```powershell
cd C:\Users\pc\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\jessicas_english_guide
```

### Step 2: Install Dependencies
```powershell
pip install -r requirements.txt
```

### Step 3: Launch Streamlit
```powershell
streamlit run app.py
```
Open your web browser at `http://localhost:8501`.
